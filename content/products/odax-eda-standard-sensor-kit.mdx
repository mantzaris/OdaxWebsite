## Overview

The **ODAX EDA Standard Sensor Kit** brings together an enclosed analog sensor, finger contact straps, and cables for electrodermal activity (EDA) and galvanic skin response (GSR) experiments.

Built around the [ODAX EDA Standard Board](/products/odax-eda-standard-board), the kit includes a black enclosure, two [ODAX EDA Contact Straps - Maker Grade - Black](/products/odax-eda-contact-strap-black-mt1), matching snap leads, and a 3.5 mm TRS cable for power, ground, and analog output.

**SKU:** `ODAX-EDA-SK1-100-BLK-R1`

It follows the enclosed-sensor approach of the [ODAX EDA Research Sensor Kit - Black](/products/odax-eda-research-sensor-kit-black), using the Standard Board's maker-grade analog circuit and MT1 contact straps. The sensor supplies an analog voltage for your own microcontroller or acquisition system to sample, log, and process.

The kit is intended for prototyping, evaluation, relative EDA/GSR trend measurement, biofeedback experiments, and embedded development. It is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, monitoring, treatment, or safety-critical use.

## The Components Inside the Kit

### ODAX EDA Standard Board

The [Standard Board](/products/odax-eda-standard-board) uses the same analog circuit and electrical routing as the ODAX EDA Maker board in a 35 × 31 mm PCB format. Its transimpedance-amplifier front end applies a low-voltage electrode bias, measures the skin-current response, and provides a ground-referenced analog output. Higher skin conductance produces higher output voltage.

- Maker-grade analog EDA/GSR signal conditioning
- Recommended 3 V to 5 V battery-powered operation
- Approximately DC to 6 Hz useful signal bandwidth for slow EDA/GSR signals
- Analog output for a high-impedance microcontroller ADC input

[View the ODAX EDA Standard Board for full electrical specifications and board details.](/products/odax-eda-standard-board)

### Maker-Grade Black Contact Straps

The two [maker-grade black contact straps](/products/odax-eda-contact-strap-black-mt1) hold Ag/AgCl electrode surfaces against the fingers with reusable hook-and-loop closures. Each strap has a nominal length of 100 mm and a 3.9 mm snap stud interface for the electrode leads.

[View the ODAX EDA Contact Strap - Maker Grade - Black for specifications, fitting, and handling guidance.](/products/odax-eda-contact-strap-black-mt1)

## Simple 3.5 mm Analog Interface

The sensor cable carries the three connections needed by the host acquisition system on a 3.5 mm TRS plug:

| TRS contact | Signal | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Tip | `VOUT` / `OUT` | Ground-referenced analog EDA/GSR output; connect to a high-impedance ADC input |
| Ring | `VIN` / `PWR` | 3.0–5.0 V DC input power |
| Sleeve | `GND` | Common ground shared with the ADC system |

The Standard Board labels its power and output connections `PWR` and `OUT`. These correspond to `VIN` and `VOUT` in the Maker board documentation.

The connector carries sensor power and signal, even though it has the familiar audio-plug shape. Do not connect it to headphones or audio equipment. Keep the output within the receiving ADC's permitted input range.

## Getting Started

1. With power off, connect the TRS tip to the host ADC input, ring to the battery-powered 3–5 V supply, and sleeve to common ground.
2. Connect the snap electrode lead set to the sensor's electrode input.
3. Attach the two snap connectors to the maker-grade contact straps and place the straps on adjacent fingers of the same hand.
4. Keep contact pressure comfortable and consistent, and support the cables to reduce movement.
5. Apply power, allow the signal to settle, and sample the analog output with a high-impedance ADC input.

Use battery power while the electrodes contact skin. Do not connect the setup to mains-referenced equipment unless the complete system has appropriate isolation designed and verified by a qualified person.

Measurement quality depends on electrode and skin condition, contact pressure, motion, ADC scaling, filtering, and calibration.
