## Overview

The **ODAX EDA Research Sensor Kit - Black** is a complete, research-oriented starting point for electrodermal activity (EDA) and galvanic skin response (GSR) acquisition.

Built around the [ODAX EDA Research Sensor Board](/products/odax-eda-research), the kit combines a precision analog front end with a black enclosure, a pair of reusable black Ag/AgCl finger contact straps, matching snap leads, and an easy three-contact output connection. Connect the sensor to a high-impedance ADC or DAQ input through a standard 3.5 mm TRS plug carrying `VOUT`, 3–5 V input power, and `GND`.

**SKU / MPN:** `ODAX-EDA-RK1-100-BLK-R1`

> **Enclosure note:** The enclosure has no brand or other exterior markings. It can be disassembled and the circuit board removed, allowing the enclosure to be repurposed.

This kit is intended for research, prototyping, evaluation, psychophysiology, HCI, affective computing, biofeedback, and custom data-acquisition workflows. It is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, monitoring, treatment, or safety-critical use.

The circuit uses carefully selected analog components and layout techniques for stable small-current measurement:

- Low-leakage **LMP7721 transimpedance-amplifier core**
- Guarded sensitive-node PCB layout for improved high-impedance behavior
- Buffered reference and excitation rails with nominal 0.5 V differential excitation
- Buffered, zero-based, ground-referenced `VOUT`
- Positive response direction: higher conductance produces higher `VOUT`
- Approximately 6.75 Hz low-frequency bandwidth for slow EDA/GSR dynamics

[See the research board product page for its complete technical details.](/products/odax-eda-research)

## Simple 3.5 mm Analog Interface

The kit exposes the three connections needed by an ADC or DAQ system on one standard 3.5 mm TRS plug:

| TRS contact | Signal | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Tip | `VOUT` | Zero-based, ground-referenced analog EDA/GSR output; connect to a high-impedance ADC or DAQ input |
| Ring | `VIN` / power | 3.0–5.0 V DC input; 3.3 V typical |
| Sleeve | `GND` | Common reference shared with the ADC or DAQ system |

> **Important:** Although the connector has the familiar 3.5 mm audio form factor, it carries sensor power and signal. Do not connect it to headphones or audio equipment.

Use a clean battery-powered or properly isolated low-noise supply while the contact straps touch skin. If the sensor is powered at 5 V and the receiving ADC accepts only 3.3 V, use appropriately reviewed input scaling or protection.

## Getting Started

1. With power off, connect the TRS tip, ring, and sleeve exactly as described above.
2. Connect the electrode lead set to the sensor enclosure.
3. Snap the two leads onto the black contact straps and place them on adjacent fingers of the same hand.
4. Apply clean, isolated 3–5 V power and allow the signal to settle.
5. Acquire `VOUT` with a high-impedance ADC or DAQ input and record the acquisition and processing settings.

For conversion guidance, nominal signal examples, and safety information, read the [ODAX EDA Research Sensor Kit Welcome Guide (PDF)](/guides/ODAX-EDA-RK1-Welcome-Pamphlet.pdf).
